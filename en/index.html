Our story

A table for the whole group.

To Tsimboloi is a meze tavern in Agia Paraskevi. The kitchen is built on familiar Greek recipes cooked over fire, in the pan, on the grill and in the clay pot, and evenings are often accompanied by live music.

Come for the meze and the wine, stay for the music. A big hall with a bar and a stage, and always a table for your group.
