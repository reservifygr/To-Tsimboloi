Privacy & Cookies (draft)

Draft text

This is draft text for the demo and is not legal advice. Before launch it must be reviewed by a lawyer and completed by the data controller.

Data controller

Legal name, VAT number, address and contact email of the venue: to be completed by the owner.

What the demo stores

  • The language you chose (tsb.lang).
  • Cart, trial orders (with name, phone and, for delivery, address), requests, images and settings, only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.
  • If you accept "analytics (demo)", simple events (e.g. page view) are recorded locally in your browser. No third-party service is loaded.
  • Instagram posts are plain links: no Instagram code loads on the page.

In production

  • Purpose and legal basis: fulfilling an order (contract) and replying to requests. Data: name, phone, order contents, delivery address, optionally email.
  • Retention period: to be defined (e.g. delete addresses and requests after a set period).
  • Rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and a complaint to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.
  • Processors (e.g. database host, email/SMS provider): to be listed with data processing agreements.
  • Cookies: strictly necessary ones need no consent. Analytics or advertising tools only after prior consent. Embedded maps or Instagram feeds load only with consent.
